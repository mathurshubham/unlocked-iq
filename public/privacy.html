Privacy

Short, plain, specific.

No accounts, no cookies, no ads, no personal tracking. Here is exactly what is stored, when, and why.

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

  • We do not ask for your email and there are no accounts.
  • We do not use cookies, advertising or third-party analytics.
  • Your result is stored only when you finish a test, so you can open it again by its link.
  • Anonymous answers are stored only if you tick the consent box.

What is stored, and when

DataWhenWhere and why
Your result: answers, questions shown, time taken, mode, optional nameWhen you finish a testOur database, so your result link works. Anyone with the link can view it, including the name if you entered one.

Progress, theme choice, practice streaksAs you use the siteYour own browser’s local storage only. It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time.

What we do not do

  • No cookies, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no fingerprinting.
  • No selling, sharing or renting of data. There is nothing to sell.
  • No emails, ever. We do not have your address.
  • We do not store your IP address in our database, and we do not link answers to a person.

Third parties

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare processes technical request data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and protect against abuse, under its own privacy policy. Fonts (Inter and Space Grotesk) are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google to fetch them. No other third-party services are used.

Retention and your choices

Results are kept for as long as the site runs, unless you ask for one to be removed. To remove a result, send the result link to the site owner and it will be deleted. Because anonymous answers carry no name or identifier, they cannot be traced back to you and so cannot be individually removed. If you do not want to contribute them, leave the consent box unticked, which is the default.

To keep a result private, leave the name blank and do not share the link. You can clear all locally stored data any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

Contact

Questions, corrections or removal requests: reach the site owner through the contact details at shubhammathur.in.
