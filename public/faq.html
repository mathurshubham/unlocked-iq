The basics

Is it really free?

Yes. The full test, the complete results, the explanations for every question and the share card are all free, and there is no premium tier. There are no ads either.

How accurate is the score?

It is an estimate for fun and self-knowledge, not a clinical assessment. It has not been normed on a representative population, and a single short test always carries measurement error, which is why you see a 90% range beside the number. Details are on the science page.

Why do I get a range instead of one number?

Because one number would claim more precision than the test has. The range shows the scores that are plausible given your answers. A narrower range means your answers pointed clearly to one level.

Can I use the result for school, a job or Mensa?

No. It is not an accepted or standardised assessment. If you need an official result, ask a qualified psychologist or an organisation that runs supervised tests.

Taking the test

Can I retake it? Will I get the same questions?

You can retake it as often as you like. Each test draws 30 questions from more than 100 question types, every one of which can generate thousands of variants, and retakes in the same browser steer away from question types you have already seening answers does not help. You will still improve a little with practice, as with any reasoning test.

What is the difference between Standard and Quick?

Standard is 30 questions in 25 minutes, with the ability to flag, skip and review before you finish. Quick is adaptive: 15 questions chosen to fit your level, about 10 minutes, no going back. Standard is the more precise of the two.

What does the test actually measure?

Fluid reasoning: spotting rules in patterns, mentally rotating and folding shapes, and finding the logic of number and letter series. It does not measure knowledge, vocabulary, memory or creativity.

Privacy and sharing

What data do you keep?

Your result is saved only when you finish, so you can open it by its link. It includes your answers, the time taken and the optional name. Anonymous answers (with no name) are stored only if you tick the consent box. There are no cookies and no trackers. The privacy page has the full list.

Do I need an account or email?

No. There are no accounts and no email field anywhere. A name is optional and only used on your results and share card.

Who can see my result?

Only people with your result link. Nothing is public or listed. If you entered a name it is part of the link page and share card, so leave it blank if you want to share a result anonymously.

Good to know

Is the test accessible?

The site works with a keyboard, respects reduced-motion and light or dark preferences, and has clear focus states. The puzzles are visual by nature, though, so people who cannot see the figures will not be able to complete most of them, and the timer may not suit everyone. Practice mode is untimed. If something blocks you, please tell us so we can improve it.

Is it suitable for children?

It is designed for adults and teenagers, and the scale is not normed for children. A score for a child will not be meaningful. Younger people can still enjoy the puzzles in practice mode.
