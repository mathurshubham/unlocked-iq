About

A test that gives you your result.

Unlocked IQ started with one small annoyance and turned into a promise: finish the test and everything is yours, immediately.

The story

It began with a paid IQ test. After thirty questions came a screen with a blurred score and a payment button. After that, the emails started: “Question #14 reveals something rare about you…”, arriving day after day, each one a nudge to pay to see a number that was already computed.

The puzzles themselves were not the problem. Reasoning puzzles are fun, and a good score feels good. The problem was treating your own result as something to ransom. So this site does the opposite: it asks for nothing and shows everything.

Every puzzle here is original and generated from published construction rules. Every result page includes your estimate, an honest range around it, the percentile, a profile of your strengths, and a plain-language reason for each question, whether you got it right or not.

Principles

Nothing locked

No paywall, no premium tier, no teaser screen. The page you see when you finish is the whole product.

Honest about accuracy

A short online test gives an estimate, not a diagnosis. You get a range and a clear explanation of what the number can and cannot tell you.

Private by default

No accounts, no email, no cookies, no ads. Your name is optional, and sharing your result is always your choice. See the privacy page.

Explained, not just scored

The point is to learn how the puzzles work. Every question explains the rule, and practice mode lets you train on as many as you like.

What this is not

It is not a clinical or standardised assessment, it has not been normed on a representative population, and it should not be used for school, job or medical decisions. It is a carefully built reasoning test for curiosity, practice and fun. If you want the details, the science page explains how the questions are built, how scoring works, and where the limits are.

Questions or corrections? Contact details are on the privacy page.
